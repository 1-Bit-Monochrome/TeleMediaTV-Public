Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2026

What TeleMedia TV Is

TeleMedia TV is an unofficial media player that uses the Telegram API. It lets you browse and play media that is already available in your own Telegram account on your TV or large-screen device.

TeleMedia TV is not affiliated with Telegram. The app does not host, provide, recommend, or distribute content.

Our Data Approach

TeleMedia TV does not collect or sell user data. Media stays on Telegram and/or your device. TeleMedia TV does not create a separate TeleMedia account, and does not sell user data.

We do not upload your Telegram media to our own servers and we do not use your content for AI training or advertising profiles.

The app works locally on your device and communicates with services that are necessary for the app to function, such as Telegram/TDLib for login and media access, Amazon Appstore or Google Play for purchases, and ad providers if rewarded ads are enabled.

Information Handled Locally by the App

The app may store or handle the following on your device so the app can work:

  • Telegram session files managed by TDLib.
  • Media cache for videos, audio, and files you choose to play.
  • Playback progress and resume positions.
  • App settings, debug settings, and local entitlement state.
  • Basic diagnostics needed to troubleshoot playback or login issues.

This information is stored locally on your device unless it is handled by Telegram, Amazon, Google, or an enabled ad provider as part of their own services.

Third-Party Services

TeleMedia TV may interact with these services when needed:

  • Telegram API / TDLib: used for Telegram login, session handling, chats, media metadata, and file access.
  • Amazon Appstore and Amazon In-App Purchasing: used for Fire TV purchases and entitlement checks.
  • Google Play Billing: used for Android TV / Google TV purchases and entitlement checks.
  • Ad providers: used only if rewarded ads are enabled.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this static policy/support website.

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies.

Local Storage and Cache

TeleMedia TV may cache selected media locally to make playback possible on TV devices. You can clear the media cache inside the app. Clearing local media cache does not delete your Telegram account or Telegram-hosted messages/media.

Support Emails

If you contact support by email, your email address and the information you choose to include will be received in our support mailbox. Do not send Telegram login codes, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive credentials.

Account and Data Deletion

You can sign out inside the app and clear media cache from Settings. TeleMedia TV cannot delete Telegram-hosted account data, messages, or media from Telegram servers. Telegram account deletion must be handled through Telegram directly.

For app-related deletion requests, contact 1bit.monochromacy@gmail.com. See our Data Deletion page for details.
