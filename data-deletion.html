Local App Data

TeleMedia TV does not collect or sell user data. Media stays on Telegram and/or your device.

Most app data is stored locally on your device or handled by services such as Telegram, Amazon Appstore, Google Play, or ad providers if enabled.

Clear Local Media Cache

You can remove local media cache inside the app:

  1. Open TeleMedia TV.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Clear Media Cache.
  4. 🎉🎉🎉

This removes local media cache used for playback. It does not delete your Telegram account or Telegram-hosted messages/media.

Sign Out

You can sign out from Telegram inside the app:

  1. Open TeleMedia TV.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Sign Out.
  4. 🎉
