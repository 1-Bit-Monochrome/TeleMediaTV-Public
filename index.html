TeleMedia TV

Unofficial media player for your Telegram media on TV.

TeleMedia TV helps you browse and play media already available in your own Telegram account on TV devices. The app does not host, provide, recommend, or distribute content.

TeleMedia TV does not collect or sell user data. Media stays on Telegram and/or your device.
